# Changelog

## throughyear 0.1.0

CRAN release: 2026-10-08

- Initial release.
- Latent multivariate-normal linking of interims to the summative scale
  with measurement error and missing interims
  ([`ty_link()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/throughyear/reference/ty_link.md)).
- Two-stage MST simulation with cold-start and prior-informed routing
  ([`ty_mst()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/throughyear/reference/ty_mst.md),
  [`ty_administer()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/throughyear/reference/ty_administer.md),
  [`ty_policies()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/throughyear/reference/ty_policies.md)).
- Fairness diagnostics by group
  ([`ty_fairness()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/throughyear/reference/ty_fairness.md)).
- Decision accuracy and consistency of through-year scores versus a
  single summative
  ([`ty_decisions()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/throughyear/reference/ty_decisions.md)).
